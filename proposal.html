1. Executive Summary

This document proposes the design and delivery of an Adaptive Multi-Agent System (MAS) for project finance modeling of mining assets with tenure-constrained financing horizons. The framework directly addresses the investment test identified in Daniel van Skye's 14 October 2026 analysis of Avonbank:

"Investors therefore need one model connecting near-term liquidity, construction funding, debt repayment and long-term equity returns."

Static deterministic DCF models and conventional spreadsheet-based project finance structures are structurally incapable of solving this problem because they:

  1. Cannot price the binary risk of a conditional 20-year licence renewal against a 36-year mine plan.
  2. Cannot dynamically reconcile immediate liquidity drains (A$3.068M annual qualifying expenditure + A$1M community funding) against construction capex.
  3. Cannot adapt to new regulatory, market, or operational data without full model rebuilds.
  4. Suffer from circular reference pathologies when debt sculpting is implemented at institutional scale.

The proposed MAS resolves all four limitations through a modular, auditable, stochastic architecture grounded in verifiable mathematics.

Rehabilitation liabilities must be rescheduled under a non-renewal scenario alongside lost production and delayed collateral release.

Agent 1 — The Core VMAR Engine (Mathematical Anchor)

Handles all deterministic and stochastic financial mathematics: cash flow waterfall (revenue → opex → tax → CFADS), non-circular debt sculpting, Real Options pricing for the 16-year extension, and rehabilitation liability scheduling.

Agent 3 — The Market & Regulatory Ingestion Agent (External Calibrator)

Uses Bayesian updating to continuously revise the probability of licence renewal at Year 20, the estimated strike price of the renewal option, and commodity price volatility surfaces.

5. Mathematical Framework

5.1 Debt Sculpting (Non-Circular)

Sculpted principal in period t:

Sculpted Principal Principalt = ( CFADSt / Target DSCR ) − Interestt

Where CFADSt is computed without reference to the debt balance, eliminating circularity.

5.2 Loan Life Coverage Ratio (Tenure-Constrained)

For senior debt, the LLCR is computed explicitly over the 20-year licence horizon, not the 36-year mine life:

LLCR (20-Year Horizon) LLCR20yr = [ Σt=1..20 CFADSt / (1 + r)t + Closing DSRA ] ÷ Opening Debt Balance

This mathematically isolates the debt capacity supported by initial-term operations.

5.3 Real Options Valuation for Licence Renewal

Years 21–36 are valued as a Real Call Option using Black-Scholes-Merton or Binomial Lattice:

Black-Scholes-Merton C = S0 N(d1) − X e−rT N(d2)
  • S0 = PV of expected FCF from Years 21–36
  • X = Strike price (re-permitting + bonding + regulatory fees at Year 20)
  • σ = Volatility from stochastic operating-margin simulations
  • T = 20 years (time to renewal decision)

5.4 Stochastic Commodity Modeling

Revenue is modeled via Geometric Brownian Motion or the Schwartz (1997) mean-reverting process:

Schwartz Mean-Reverting Process dS = κ ( μ − ln S ) S dt + σ S dW

Input costs use correlated mean-reverting processes. Monte Carlo simulations (with variance reduction) produce stable P50/P90/P99 distributions for DSCR, LLCR, and equity IRR.

5.5 Funding Gap Calculation

Funding Gap Funding Gap = Capex + WC + Σt=1..3 Mandatory Expendituret − Committed Equity

Stress-tested across 10,000+ Monte Carlo paths to quantify probability of funding shortfall.

6. VMAR Architecture Principles

All deliverables adhere to strict Verifiable, Modular, Auditable, Reliable (VMAR) standards:

  1. Modularity: Strict separation of the calculation engine (cash flow, debt, tax, option pricing) from the user interface. No circular references.
  2. Verifiability: Every formula is unit-tested against known analytical solutions. Monte Carlo outputs are validated via variance reduction and convergence testing.
  3. Auditability: Full version control, parameter logging, and reproducible seeds for all stochastic runs.
  4. Reliability: Production-grade code (Python / C++), not spreadsheet macros. Designed for institutional deployment.
